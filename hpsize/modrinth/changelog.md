First release for Minecraft 1.21.11 (Fabric).

- Every mob is as big as its health: 20 HP is normal size, every hit shrinks the mob.
- `/hpsize` with presets (`film`, `smooth`, `fair`, `light`) and per-world settings.
- Commands for setting up shots: `/spawnsized`, `/mobhp`, `/heal`, `/glow`, `/mute`.
- Commands for recording videos: `/go`, `/countdown`, `/freeze`, `/announce`, `/recmode`, `/cam`, `/nv`, `/cleanup`.
- The Ender Dragon is ×8 at full health (model and hitbox parts), `/hpsize dragon <size>`.
- Clean HUD key (`H`) on the client.
- English and Polish.
